Frequently asked questions

Document translation questions.

What types of documents can Davila & Associates translate?

We translate legal, certified, academic, business, healthcare, government and personal documents, including birth and marriage certificates, court records, contracts, transcripts and correspondence.

Do you provide certified document translation?

Yes. A certification statement can be provided when requested. The receiving organization should confirm its current submission requirements.

Do you translate Spanish to English and English to Spanish?

Yes. Spanish-to-English and English-to-Spanish document translation are core services, with additional language pairs available based on the project.

Can I order document translation from outside Texas?

Yes. Document translation can be coordinated remotely for clients throughout the United States.

How do I send my documents?

After you submit a translation service request, we provide a secure upload step for the source files.
