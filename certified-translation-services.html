Certified translation

What does “certified” mean?

A certified translation comes with a signed statement. It identifies the translated document and states that the translation is complete and accurate.

  • Professional document translation
  • Certification statement provided with the translation
  • Formatting designed to make the translated document clear and usable
  • Project handling based on the receiving organization’s stated requirements

Important: Ask the office receiving your records what it needs before you order. Send those instructions with your request.
