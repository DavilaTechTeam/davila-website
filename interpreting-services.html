Planning an assignment

The details matter before the interpreter arrives.

Providing complete assignment information helps us coordinate the right interpreter and avoid delays.

  • Language and dialect
  • Date, time and expected duration
  • In-person location or remote platform
  • Type of proceeding or meeting
  • Names and terminology when appropriate
  • Any special access or security requirements

Remote and in-person planning

Choose the setting before confirming an interpreter.

Remote interpreting can support clients across the United States. In-person assignments depend on location, language, schedule and interpreter availability.

Remote meetings

Provide the time zone, platform, host contact and expected number of speakers. Tell us whether participants will join from separate devices and whether the meeting includes presentations or audience questions.

On-site assignments

Include the full address, parking or access instructions, start time and expected duration. Tell us about room layout, equipment and any check-in requirements when requesting availability.

Match the service to the communication

For depositions and hearings, request legal interpreting services. If you need a written file translated instead of live language support, use document translation services.
