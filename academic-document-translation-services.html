Keep the record clear and complete.

Academic documents often contain structured information that must remain easy to review.

  • Names and dates
  • Course titles and grades
  • Institution names
  • Degree or certificate information
  • Visible seals and annotations
  • Certification when requested

Transcripts and diplomas

Make the academic record easy for the recipient to review.

A school record can include course tables, grading keys, institutional seals and notes on the reverse. Send those details together with the transcript or diploma.

Include the grading key

Send any page explaining grades, credits, abbreviations or the academic calendar. Keep pages in order and identify which diploma belongs with each transcript. This helps preserve the relationship between the records.

Separate translation from evaluation

Translation communicates the source record in another language. Credential evaluation assesses how that qualification compares with another education system. Ask the school or evaluator which services and submission method it requires.

Coordinate a complete academic packet

List the receiving institution, application deadline and records to be translated. For Spanish source documents, see Spanish to English translation services. Add any certification instructions to the same request.
