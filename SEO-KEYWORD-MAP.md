## Ownership and internal links

- Use the homepage for the broad nationwide proposition; use the document hub to help visitors choose written-document services.
- Use the certified service page for ordering and certification logistics. Use the certified-translation guide for definitions and preparation questions, with a link to the service page.
- Use general interpreting for meeting formats and logistics; use legal interpreting for depositions and proceedings. Keep written legal translation on its own page.
- Link to the most specific useful destination with descriptive anchor text. Mentioning a related service is appropriate; avoid making every page chase the same phrase.
- Keep the McAllen page local to the actual office. Nationwide coordination does not imply offices or in-person staffing in every state.
- Keep redirects, the confirmation page, error pages and /index.html out of the keyword map. The homepage canonical is /.
