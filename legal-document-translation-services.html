Written case materials

Set the scope before a case file moves into translation.

Legal document translation covers written material. A case may also need a live interpreter or deposition video; identify each service so scheduling and document deadlines can be coordinated.

Keep exhibit references clear

Provide the file names, page order, exhibit labels and any numbering that should remain visible. Identify priority records and tell us if related documents will arrive in stages.

Share useful terminology

Send approved spellings of party names and any existing glossary or related translation. Identify the document version and its intended reader so terminology can be reviewed consistently across the file set.

Coordinate the live proceeding separately

Use legal interpreting services for spoken communication at a deposition, hearing or attorney meeting. For official statements accompanying written translations, review certified translation services.

Frequently asked questions

Legal document translation questions.

What types of legal documents can you translate?

We translate court records, contracts, affidavits, pleadings, evidence, deposition materials, correspondence and other legal documents.

Do you translate legal documents from Spanish to English?

Yes. Spanish-to-English and English-to-Spanish legal translation are core services. Additional language pairs may also be available.

Can legal document translations be certified?

Yes, certification can be provided when requested. The receiving court, agency or organization should confirm its current requirements for the specific matter.

Can law firms send multiple documents for an active case?

Yes. We can coordinate multi-document and recurring legal translation projects. Provide the deadline, intended use and any priority documents when submitting the request.

Can you also provide an interpreter for the same matter?

Yes. Davila & Associates provides professional interpreting for depositions, hearings, mediations, meetings and other legal proceedings.

Do you provide legal videography?

Yes. Legal videography is one of our core service lines and can be coordinated with interpreting when a proceeding requires both services.
