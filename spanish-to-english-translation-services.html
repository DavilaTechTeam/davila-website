Preparing your source documents

Small details can change how a record is understood.

Provide an uncropped scan or a clear photograph of each page. Check that document edges, stamps and handwritten annotations are visible. For records with information on both sides, include the reverse.

  • Keep documents grouped by person or matter.
  • Identify the receiving court, school, agency or business.
  • Share its written translation and certification instructions.
  • Flag inconsistent names or dates for review.
  • Tell us the submission deadline before work begins.

We translate what the record says; translation does not change or correct the underlying official document.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about translating Spanish records into English.

Can I send a Spanish document from another state?

Yes. Clients throughout the United States can start with our online request form. Tell us the document type and deadline; our team will coordinate the secure upload and delivery details without requiring an office visit.

Can you translate a Mexican birth certificate into English?

Yes. Include a clear copy of the entire certificate, including the reverse side if it contains information. Let us know where you will submit the translation and whether that organization requests a signed certification statement.

What happens to stamps, handwritten notes and illegible text?

Include every page and margin so we can review seals, stamps and handwritten entries in context. If part of the source cannot be read, we may request a clearer copy or clarification. Missing or unclear information should never be guessed.

Is translating a transcript the same as evaluating a foreign degree?

No. Translation presents the Spanish-language record in English. A credential evaluation determines how qualifications compare with another education system. Tell us if your receiving school or evaluator has instructions for the translation.

How is the price for Spanish-to-English translation determined?

We review the number of pages or words, legibility, subject matter, layout, certification needs and requested delivery date. Submit your project details for a quote; there is no single price that fits every record or document set.

Can you coordinate several records for the same application?

Yes. Identify which documents belong together and provide any spelling preferences supported by your official records. Tell us about a shared deadline so the translation scope and delivery can be coordinated.

Need a Spanish document translated into English?

Tell us the document type, deadline and intended use. We’ll help you with the next step.
