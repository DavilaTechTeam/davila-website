Certified document translation

What should you send us?

Send the clearest and most complete copy available. Include the entire document, including reverse sides when they contain information, and avoid cropping stamps, seals, signatures or handwritten annotations.

Before uploading

Make sure the document is readable and complete. If you were given specific instructions by the receiving organization, include those instructions with your request.

Need more than a birth certificate?

We also translate marriage certificates, divorce records, academic records, legal documents, business documents and other official records.

Names and record details

Keep spellings and annotations connected to the source record.

Birth records may use more than one surname, handwritten entries or registration notes. Clear source files help us review each detail in context.

Flag spelling differences early

If the name on a passport or another record differs from the birth certificate, identify the difference in your request. Include any preferred English spelling supplied by the recipient so it can be reviewed alongside the source.

Include later entries

Send marginal notes, amendments and separate pages that belong to the certificate. A short-form certificate and a full registry record can contain different information; tell us which version your recipient requested.

Tell us the purpose

A school, employer or agency may give its own submission instructions. For several records connected to an immigration matter, use immigration document translation services to describe the complete packet.
