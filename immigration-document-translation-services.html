Organize the document packet

Keep related records together without losing their identity.

Start with a list of the documents you need translated and the instructions you received. Our role is to translate the records; your attorney or receiving agency can identify which records the matter requires.

List each record

Identify the person, document type, source language and page count. Separate birth certificates, marriage and divorce records and academic documents in the file list.

Provide the complete source

Include reverse sides, seals, amendments and handwritten notes. Keep unrelated files in separate groups and identify duplicate copies so the scope can be reviewed before work begins.

Include the actual deadline

Tell us when the translation is needed and whether any records have priority. Send certification and delivery instructions with the request. See certified translation services for the statement and delivery questions to confirm.

Frequently asked questions

Immigration document translation questions.

Do you translate documents for immigration-related matters?

Yes. We translate birth certificates, marriage certificates, divorce records, affidavits, identity records and other supporting documents commonly used in immigration-related matters.

Can immigration document translations be certified?

Yes. A certification statement can be provided when requested. Clients should confirm the current requirements of the receiving agency for their specific matter.

Do you translate Spanish documents into English?

Yes. Spanish-to-English document translation is a core service, with other language pairs available based on the project.

Can you guarantee that a translation will be accepted by USCIS or another agency?

No company can guarantee an agency decision. We provide professional translation and certification services, while the receiving agency determines its own current acceptance requirements.

Can I order remotely from another state?

Yes. Immigration-related document translation can be coordinated remotely for clients throughout the United States.
