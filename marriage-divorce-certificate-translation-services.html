Frequently asked questions

Marriage and divorce translation questions.

Can you translate a marriage certificate from Spanish to English?

Yes. Spanish-to-English and English-to-Spanish marriage certificate translation are core services. Additional language pairs may also be available.

Can divorce decrees and divorce certificates be translated?

Yes. We translate divorce decrees, divorce certificates, judgments and related court or civil records.

Can marriage and divorce translations be certified?

Yes. A certification statement can be provided when requested. Confirm any specific requirements with the receiving agency or organization.

Can these documents be translated for immigration-related use?

Yes. Marriage and divorce records are commonly translated for immigration-related matters. The receiving agency’s current requirements should be confirmed for the specific case.

Can I order translation if I am outside Texas?

Yes. Document translation can be coordinated remotely for clients throughout the United States.

Should I send every page of a divorce decree?

Yes. Send the complete document, including all pages and any reverse sides that contain information, stamps, seals or annotations.
