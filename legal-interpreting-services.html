Prepare for the deposition

Confirm whether the proceeding is remote or on site. Share names, relevant terminology and permitted preparation material in advance. Use our deposition interpreter preparation guide to organize the details.

Distinguish spoken and written work

Live interpreting and document translation require separate project details. If exhibits, affidavits or other written records also need translation, include a request for legal document translation services.

Coordinate recording needs

If video recording is required, include the location, start time and expected length with the legal videography services request. For meetings outside a legal proceeding, see general interpreting services.

Frequently asked questions

Legal interpreting questions.

Do you provide interpreters for depositions?

Yes. We arrange interpreters for depositions. Ask about on-site or remote service for your matter.

Do you provide court and hearing interpreters?

Yes. We arrange interpreters for hearings and other legal matters. We review the court’s requirements and confirm who is available.

Can you coordinate legal videography with an interpreter?

Yes. You can request both video and an interpreter for the same deposition or legal proceeding.

What information should I provide when requesting a legal interpreter?

Tell us the language and dialect, date, time and expected length. Include the type of proceeding, the location or remote platform, and any key terms or access details.

How early should I book a legal interpreter?

Book as early as you can. Allow more time for less common languages, long proceedings or a fixed court schedule.

Tell us the language, proceeding, date, time, location and expected duration.
